# Changelog

## 2.0.1
- Fixed the homepage showing a 404 after uploading the theme: Shopify rejected the Bento grid section because its Columns on desktop slider had only two values, which also stopped the homepage template from loading. The slider now goes from 2 to 4 columns.

## 2.0.0
- New design: Valise now presents bags like objects in a gallery, with its own homepage, header, product cards, product page, collection page and footer.
- New sections: Rooms (one bag per screen, the page takes each room's color), Product room (the product page as a room with a museum label, tinted by the chosen color), Product views, Wall text, Catalogue entry, Hall (a salon wall of products), Page intro, Visitor book, Gallery header (with a full-screen Index) and Colophon footer.
- Product cards: a new Museum label layout, on by default.
- Collection page: Salon wall grid option and a setting to hide the title when a Page intro is used.
- Five new presets: Gallery, Nocturne, Terracotta, Salon and Stone.
- New fonts: Gloock and EB Garamond, with Aref Ruqaa and Scheherazade New for Arabic.
- Missing photos show drawn leather bags on plinths.
- The previous slideshow, header and footer are still available as sections.

## 1.0.0
- First release of Valise, a theme for handbag, leather goods and travel bag brands.
- Five style presets: Valise, Cognac, Noir, Voyage and Bordeaux.
- Bundled fonts: Josefin Sans, Newsreader and Marcellus for headings, Figtree for text, with IBM Plex Sans Arabic and Markazi Text for Arabic.
- Slideshow: zoom reveal between slides, text in a thin frame that draws itself, slides with two photos side by side, a second button, and a next button with a timer ring and the slide number.
- New section: A closer look, a pinned bag photo whose numbered points, lines and labels appear one by one as shoppers scroll. Phones show the points as a numbered list.
- New product blocks: Bag size (measurements with a drawing, what fits inside and ways to carry, all ready for metafields) and Initials (a live leather tag preview, optional foil choices, saved on the order).
- Featured products: large underlined words as tabs, and a View all button under the products.
- Header: country and currency selector on desktop. Announcement bar: message at the start and a countdown at the end, with labels under the numbers.
- Product cards: badges as small text, and new icons for features and accordions (bag, wallet, key, laptop, personalization).
- Missing photos show line drawings of bags in studio light, matched to the product name when it says the bag type.
- Includes every section from the shared engine.
